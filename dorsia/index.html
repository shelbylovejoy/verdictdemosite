Why I'd build it

The problem, what's already there, how I'd test it, and why me.

01

One card pays for the whole table

Dorsia already took the check out of dinner: the minimum is prepaid, the check closes to Dorsia, and you leave when you're done. That's the best part of the product.

But the prepaid minimum, and any overage, tax and tip, all land on the member's card. For a table of four at a $150 minimum, the host fronts $600 and hopes everyone pays them back. Guests also can't drop off the booking without the host still owing the full minimum.

Problem framing
1 card
covers the prepaid minimum, overage, tax and tip for the whole table today, and it's the member's (Dorsia restaurant playbook).
02

Dorsia is already heading here

  • Members prepay a per-person minimum. At the end, Dorsia charges the member's card for overage, tax and tip. Party size can go up after booking but not down, and the minimum stays the same if fewer people show.

    Dorsia playbook

  • When Dorsia raised $50.4 million, it said it planned an Uber-like prepayment feature for diners. Splitting that prepayment is the natural next step.

    Refresh Miami, Feb 2025

  • Every guest who pays from a link is a person who's seen Dorsia work. With about 125,000 members and 50,000 people waiting to join, that's a strong top of the funnel.

    Refresh Miami, Feb 2025
Research
03

Start with tables of four or more

Bigger tables are where fronting the minimum hurts most. I'd turn this on for bookings of four or more in New York and Miami and compare them with similar bookings without it.

Measure

  • Group bookings where guests pay by link
  • Days until the host is fully paid back

Watch

  • Guests who apply for membership
  • Repeat bookings by hosts

Guardrails

  • Restaurant payouts don't change
  • No-shows don't rise
Experiment design
04

I design for the guest first

At Paramount I built Verdict. The host has the TV and the subscription. Everyone else just has a phone, so guests join from the browser, with no app and no account. A Dorsia guest paying from a link is the same idea.
