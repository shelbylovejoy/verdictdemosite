Why I'd build it

The problem, what's already there, how I'd test it, and why me.

01

Everyone in the lecture makes the same deck

In a big college course, hundreds of students turn the same lectures into flashcards, alone, the week before the exam. The best cards stay in one person's account.

Gizmo is already social. You can share decks by link and play live Whiz games with friends. What's missing is the class itself, which is how college students actually organize studying.

Research
03

Start with big intro courses

Large intro courses at a handful of US universities have the most students making the same cards. I'd launch class decks there, timed to the first round of midterms, and compare with students who study solo.

Measure

  • New users who join a class deck
  • Cards studied in the week before an exam

Watch

  • Students who come back next semester
  • Upgrades in exam weeks

Guardrails

  • Only student-made cards get shared
  • Card quality ratings stay high
Experiment design
