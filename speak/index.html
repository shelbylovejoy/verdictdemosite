Practice for the place you're going.

A lot of people learning Spanish have a trip or a semester on the calendar. Speak could practice the Spanish they'll actually hear there, and count down to the day they land.

Why I'd build it

The problem, what's already there, how I'd test it, and why me.

01

Learners have a place and a date. The app doesn't know either.

People rarely learn Spanish in general. They learn it for Madrid in January, or a job in Mexico City. 298,180 U.S. students studied abroad for credit in 2023/24, and Spain was the second most popular destination.

Spanish in Madrid doesn't sound like Spanish in Mexico City, and the words for everyday things change. One review this year says Speak's Spanish defaults to Latin American Spanish, and another found no way to choose a regional accent or voice.

Problem framing

02

Speak already has the engine

  • Speak already builds lessons around real-life scenarios and adapts them to the learner. A destination and a date are two more inputs to the same engine.

    Speak on the App Store

  • One review says Speak's Spanish defaults to Latin American Spanish. Another found no choice of regional accent or voice.

    LanguaTalk, Feb 2026 · LiveLingo, Jul 2026

  • Speak's CEO has said efficacy comes before engagement. A real date on the calendar is the most honest reason to practice.

    TechCrunch, Dec 2024

  • The demand is growing. U.S. study abroad was up 6% in 2023/24, and Spain hit a record.

    IIE Open Doors, Nov 2025
Research
03

Start with Spanish learners who have a date

Ask new Spanish learners one optional question: going anywhere soon? Give the people who say yes the countdown, and compare them with similar learners who don't get it.

Measure

  • Days practiced in the four weeks before the trip
  • Countdown roleplays finished

Watch

  • Learners who keep going after they land
  • Annual plans bought by people with a date

Guardrails

  • Learners without a trip get the same course as today
  • Speech scoring stays as accurate in every region
Experiment design
04
