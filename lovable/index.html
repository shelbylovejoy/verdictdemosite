Why I'd build it

The problem, what's already there, how I'd test it, and why me.

01

It works for the builder, which isn't the test

Lovable makes it possible for people who've never written code to ship real software, and a lot of them want it to become a business. Payments launched in February 2026, so the next question for many builders is whether strangers will actually use the thing and pay for it.

The builder knows exactly where every button is. The first person who doesn't is the one who finds the problems, and right now that test happens outside Lovable, if it happens at all.

Problem framing
80%
of people building on Lovable are in non-technical roles, and about 8 in 10 plan to make money from what they build (Lovable report, June 2026, via The Next Web).
02

Why I think it works

  • About 80% of Lovable builders self-identify as non-technical. More than half say they're building a business, and another quarter have side projects they want to turn into income.

    The Next Web, Jun 2026

  • Lovable passed $500M in annualized revenue, and projects built on it average 720 million visits a month. Payments launched in February 2026.

    The Next Web, Jun 2026

  • Small tests go a long way. Jakob Nielsen found that a first test with five people turns up about 85% of usability problems, so five testers is a realistic first round.

    Nielsen Norman Group
Research
03

Start with builders about to launch

Builders who just connected payments or a custom domain are about to put their project in front of strangers. I'd offer them a test and compare launches with similar builders who don't get one.

Measure

  • Tests sent
  • Fixes applied from a test

Watch

  • Projects that publish within 30 days
  • Payments revenue for tested projects

Guardrails

  • Testers never need an account
  • Test links expire when the test closes
Experiment design
04

I've run the playtest

At Paramount I built Verdict and playtested it with 8 interns. Their biggest ask was picking by mood, so I rebuilt the picker around moods.
