Why I'd build it

The problem, what's already there, how I'd test it, and why me.

01

The hard part happens at the gate

Buying a Holafly eSIM is easy. Activating it is where people get stuck, and it happens right when they've landed and need a ride, a map or a message home.

One long-time Holafly customer's 2026 review lists the usual problems: data roaming left off, trying to activate before leaving home, network settings that don't fill in, and deleting the eSIM by mistake. Each one is something the app could check or prevent.

02

Holafly already sells the peace of mind

  • Holafly's chief product officer describes the product as peace of mind and effortless setup, and mentions travelers who forget to turn their plan back on after landing.

    Pulse 2.0 interview with Jon Ross, Jun 2026

  • Activation needs data roaming on, won't work before you reach the destination, sometimes needs manual network settings, and uses a single-use QR code.

    Adam and Linds review, Aug 2026

  • Scale makes small fixes count. Holafly has sold more than 15 million eSIMs across 200 destinations and runs support around the clock in 18 languages, so every question the app answers itself is a chat saved.

    Holafly, Sep 2025
Research
03

Start with first-time customers

People buying their first eSIM are the most likely to get stuck. I'd turn this on for new customers with a trip in the next 30 days and compare them with new customers who don't get it.

Measure

  • Support chats per 100 activations
  • Share online within 30 minutes of landing

Watch

  • Second purchases
  • Holafly Plans subscriptions

Guardrails

  • No extra steps for people who already know how
  • Refund requests don't rise
