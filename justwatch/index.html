Why I'd build it

The problem, what's already there, how I'd test it, and why me.

01

Where to watch is solved. What to watch isn't.

JustWatch tracks more than 500,000 movies and shows across 1,500+ services. The harder question is what to watch, and it gets harder with more people on the couch. Viewers browse about 12 minutes before picking something, and 46% say finding something is getting harder.

One person holds the remote and scrolls while everyone else says they don't care, then vetoes. A streaming service can only fix that with its own catalog. JustWatch can fix it with all of them.

Problem framing
12 min

02

Why now

  • JustWatch TV opens to users this month, with free, rental and ad-free titles inside the JustWatch apps. The pick and the play can finally happen in the same place.

    Cord Cutters News, Sep 2026 · PPC Land, Sep 2026

  • The TV apps are already there. JustWatch has been on Apple TV, Fire TV and Android TV since 2019, after users asked for it almost daily.

    Cord Cutters News, Dec 2019

  • No streamer can build this one. Each streamer's picker only knows its own catalog. JustWatch sees more than 1,500 services, so its deck can cover everything the household pays for.

    PPC Land, Sep 2026

  • The audience is already there. More than 50 million people use JustWatch every month.

    Cord Cutters News, Sep 2026
Research
03

Launch it with JustWatch TV

I'd start in the US TV apps with households that have two or more services connected, and compare them with similar households that don't get it.

Measure

  • Seconds from opening the app to playing something
  • Group sessions that end in playback

Watch

  • JustWatch TV plays that start from a group pick
  • Guests who come back to JustWatch on their own

Guardrails

  • Click-outs to partner services don't drop
  • Solo browsing doesn't get slower
Experiment design
04

I've built a version of this already

At Paramount I built Verdict, a game that helps a room pick a movie. The TV hosts, everyone joins from their phone's browser with no app, swipes the same ten movies, and the TV shows the top three. It could only pick from one streaming catalog. JustWatch could pick from all of them.
