I grew up in Texas and study computer science at Georgia Tech. In every group chat, I’m the idea-maker. I have the vision, and I want to build it and experience it.

I got into product because I love designing for the person on the other end. The first product I built from idea to demo was Verdict, a game that gets a whole room to agree on what to watch.

At Paramount+ in summer 2026, I was handed a cleanup of 10 feature flags. I treated it as a discoverability problem instead and built a tool that audits all 385 of them. It found 90 that were dead or stale. I also turned more than 25 discovery conversations across engineering, product and design into a map of how a 200-plus-person org takes work from discovery to release.

This fall I’m a Product Fellow with Product@GT, leading the Brazil research in a five-country competitive assessment for UPS. Before that, I built a computer vision pipeline with HumaniTech that tells individual foxes apart, for a team working toward automated rabies vaccine distribution in Atlanta. I also help run Startup Exchange events at Georgia Tech, which reached a record 3,000+ participants in one semester.

I’m also really, really into travel, so my passport is getting full. On the side, I make UGC for brands.
