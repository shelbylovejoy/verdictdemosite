Why I'd build it

The problem, what's already there, how I'd test it, and why me.

01

Picking takes longer than making

Krea's whole point is that exploring is fast, so a designer ends up with a lot of strong directions. Then the decision goes to someone outside Krea, usually as a deck, a thread or a folder of screenshots, and the feedback comes back in five different places.

As Krea moves into bigger teams and companies, more of the work is getting other people to agree. That part could happen where the images already are.

Problem framing
74%
of US marketing and advertising pros say approvals take more effort than the creative work itself (StreamWork survey of 500, run by Pollfish, June 2026).
02

Why I think it works

  • Krea has raised $83M at a reported $500M valuation, counts creators at Pixar, LEGO and Samsung as users, and said its Series B would go toward enterprise features.

    TechCrunch, Apr 2025

  • Krea is already building for teams. Team plans share compute across members, and Krea Agent is rolling out to creative teams' workspaces starting June 2026.

    Krea, 2026

  • In the same survey, over 60% said they spend up to a full workday each week chasing approvals, with feedback scattered across email, Slack, annotated PDFs and calls. It comes from a company that sells approval software, so I'd treat the exact numbers with some caution.

    StreamWork, Jun 2026
Research
03

Start with teams that already send work out

Team workspaces that download and send images out the most are already doing reviews somewhere else. I'd give them review links and compare their projects with similar teams.

Measure

  • Review links sent
  • New rounds started from winners

Watch

  • Seats added to team plans
  • Time from first image to final pick

Guardrails

  • Links are private and expire
  • Reviewers only see the shared set
Experiment design
04

I've built a picker before

At Paramount I built Verdict, a game where everyone swipes the same set on their own phone and the group lands on one answer. Picking one image out of twenty with a client is the same problem as picking one movie out of ten with a room.
