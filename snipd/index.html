A concept for Snipd · Unofficial

Start a podcast club.

Snipd makes saving the best moment of a podcast a single tap. Those moments mostly go to a notes app or out as a one-off share. A small club, where friends pick one episode a week and see each other's snips on the timeline, would turn saving into talking.

Why I'd build it

The problem, what's already there, how I'd test it, and why me.

01

Snips stop at the notes app

Snipd turns a headphone tap into a clip, a transcript and a summary, then sends it to Notion, Readwise or a notes app. That's great for remembering. It doesn't help when the best part of an episode is arguing about it with a friend who heard it too.

People already share snips as quote cards, one friend at a time. A club gives those shares a home, and gives friends a reason to listen to the same episode in the same week.

Why I think it works

  • Snipd saves ideas with a tap of your headphones, with an AI transcript and summary, and syncs them to Notion and Readwise.

    Snipd on the App Store

  • Snips are already shareable. A 2026 review highlights quote cards that make it easy to pass highlights to friends and colleagues.

    AI Indigo, 2026

  • Other people's highlights already help. You can sample highlights others have made from shows you don't follow, which is a way to find new podcasts.

    Wonder Tools
Research
03

Start with the people who share most

Listeners who already share snips with friends have the habit. I'd let them start clubs and compare them with similar listeners who don't get clubs.

Measure

  • Clubs started
  • Members who snip the club episode

Watch

  • 30-day retention for club members
  • Premium upgrades

Guardrails

  • Club snips stay inside the club
  • At most one notification per episode
Experiment design
04

I've built for picking one thing together

At Paramount I built Verdict, where a room picks one thing to watch together and everyone ends up with the same thing to talk about. A podcast club is that, spread over a week.
