A concept for Freeletics · Unofficial

Train with a friend.

Freeletics started as a community of people who met up and pushed each other through the same workouts. Today the Coach is personal and the training is solo. A training buddy, doing the same session at their own level, would bring the friend back without bringing back a feed.

Why I'd build it

The problem, what's already there, how I'd test it, and why me.

01

The community went quiet

Freeletics grew as a community. People met in local groups and pushed each other through the same workouts. The app has since become a personal AI Coach, which is better for most days, but the friend part faded. Training spots were removed, and in 2024 Freeletics told users on its forum that it wasn't working on bringing them back.

Users in that thread said the Facebook groups people used instead had gone mostly inactive. A buddy feature is a small, private version of what they were asking for.

Problem framing
41M+
people had used Freeletics by 2020, and many of them met up in local groups to train together (Fit Tech interview with CEO Daniel Sobhani).
02

Why I think it works

  • In 2024, users asked Freeletics to bring back training groups or Training spots, so they could find people to train with. Freeletics said it wasn't currently working on it.

    Freeletics forum, Mar 2024

  • The Coach already personalizes every workout from feedback after each session, which is what makes one shared session at two different levels possible.

    Fit Tech, 2020

  • In a study of 153 college students, people exercising alongside a partner held plank exercises longer than people exercising alone, even when the partner was only virtually present.

    Forlenza et al., Games for Health Journal, 2012
Research
03

Start with people who already train most days

People who train four or more days a week have the habit, and a buddy would help them keep it. I'd offer them a buddy and compare with similar users who train solo.

Measure

  • Buddy invites accepted
  • Sessions per week for each buddy

Watch

  • 90-day retention
  • Subscriptions started by invited buddies

Guardrails

  • Buddies never see body stats
  • At most one nudge a day
Experiment design
04

Separate phones, one shared result

At Paramount I built Verdict, where everyone takes part from their own phone and the group gets one shared answer. A training buddy is the same idea for two people: two separate workouts, one shared week.
