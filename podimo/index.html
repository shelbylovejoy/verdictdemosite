Why I'd build it

The problem, what's already there, how I'd test it, and why me.

01

The car is where podcasts lose

Plenty of listening happens in the car, and it's where podcasts do worst. One person holds the phone, everyone else gets whatever that person likes, and on a family drive it's easier to just put on the radio.

Podimo's shows are already built for taking part, with polls, comments and in-show reactions. A road trip mode would bring that into the car, where the audience is sitting right next to each other.

Problem framing
5%
of in-car audio time goes to podcasts, the lowest share of anywhere people listen (Edison Research Share of Ear, Q2 2023).
02

Why I think it works

  • Podimo passed 1 million paying subscribers in five years, across six markets in Europe and Latin America. Shows already have comments, in-show emojis and polls, and listening grew 45% in 2024 after interface upgrades.

    Podimo via Podnews, Feb 2025

  • Podcasts get 14% of audio time at work and 12% at home, but only 5% in the car, the one place where they fall below 10%.

    Edison Research via SSRS, Jul 2023

  • Families already come to Podimo for age-appropriate kids' content, and a review analysis names the kids' library as a growth lever. A family drive is where kids and parents listen together.

    Marlvel, May 2026
Research
03

Start with a holiday weekend

I'd turn on Road trip in one market before a holiday weekend, for subscribers who already listen in the car, and compare them with similar listeners who don't get it.

Measure

  • Road trips started
  • Votes per trip

Watch

  • Listening minutes on drive days
  • Trials started by passengers

Guardrails

  • The driver never has to touch the phone
  • Kids mode keeps explicit shows out
Experiment design
04

I've built this for a living room

At Paramount I built Verdict. The TV hosts, everyone joins from their phone's browser with no app and no account, and the TV shows the top three. A car screen and a few phones is the same setup with seatbelts.

I'm a CS student at Georgia Tech, and I'd like to spend next summer building at Podimo.
